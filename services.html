FAQ

We’ve heard it all. Here’s everything you need to know before working with us.

How long does it typically take to complete a custom build?

It depends on how much you want to add to your scope and the complexity of the build. Simpler Shopify to Shopify builds can take as little as 2 months, if you're happy to take our lead on design and customer experience. For larger, more complex migrations, and if you're wanting a lot more customisation and revisions, it can take up to 5 months.

What exactly is included in the theme and custom builds service?

For our base offering, we focus on delivering X amount of core pages customised to your brand and your customers. We also deliver X system pages which are built to Shopify's standard settings and structure. You can add additional functions and pages into your scope should you want it, so you can really tailor your build to what works for you!

Who do we work with at Vinova?

Our eCommerce clients are forward-thinking brands that strive to make a lasting impression by balancing exceptional design with seamless functionality. These businesses are focused on scaling rapidly, typically generating annual revenues starting at £5 million. We collaborate across diverse industries, including fashion, beauty, home goods, gifts, sports, outdoor activities, and food and beverage, delivering tailored solutions to meet their unique needs.

Are there any additional costs associated with custom features?

Yes, we offer base packages which you can add any plugins which deliver the features you need, or if no app covers what you need we can custom build functionality.

Can I manage my website and make updates after it's built?

Yes! Your website will be designed for easy updates, allowing you to add products, edit content, and create new pages whenever you need. We’ll provide comprehensive training so you can confidently manage your store and continue growing your brand independently, without relying on a retainer.
